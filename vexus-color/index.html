Quick reference

Hepatic vein

  • Abdominal preset, scale about 40 cm/s. Blue = flow away from the probe toward the IVC.
  • Gate in any hepatic vein, 1 to 2 cm from the IVC junction.
  • Read with the ECG: A after P, S with the QRS, V at end systole, D after the T wave.
  • S > D normal; S < D mild; S reversal severe.

Portal vein

  • Red = flow toward the probe (hepatopetal). A mosaic vessel beside it is the hepatic artery.
  • Gate distally within the portal vein, away from the artery. Lower the PW scale.
  • PVPF = (Vmax − Vmin) ÷ Vmax × 100.
  • Under 30% normal; 30 to 49% mild; 50% or more severe.

Intrarenal vein

  • Abdominal preset, color scale under 20 cm/s, raise color gain slowly.
  • Gate an interlobar pair: artery above the baseline, vein below.
  • Continuous normal; biphasic S and D mild; D only (monophasic) severe.
  • Hardest view; not validated in CKD or kidney transplant.
